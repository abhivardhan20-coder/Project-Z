# Project Z asset provenance

The Godot runtime is distributed under the MIT License. The official license and third-party notices are available from [Godot Engine](https://godotengine.org/license/) and [the Godot source repository](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/master/COPYRIGHT.txt).

The GLBs and their embedded textures came from the existing local Project Z asset library. The supplied copy did not contain a third-party rights manifest or Blender source files for these assets. The package is a development deliverable for that workspace. Public redistribution rights for the local library require verification.

The city shaders, generated environment geometry, UI and synthesized audio were created in this workspace. The game uses installed Windows fonts when available and the Godot fallback otherwise. No font files or commercial audio recordings are bundled.

The following assets were downloaded from Poly Haven under [CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license). The surface maps are the 1K JPEG diffuse variants. Model textures were embedded into the downloaded glTF geometry for the standalone build.

- [Asphalt Track](https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_track), with its 1K OpenGL normal map.
- [Leafy Grass](https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass)
- [Aerial Grass Rock](https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock)
- [Fire Hydrant](https://polyhaven.com/a/fire_hydrant)
- [Concrete Road Barrier 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_road_barrier_02)

Mercy Clinic uses the [Hospital Bed](https://grab3d.com/models/furniture/hospital-bed/) from Grab3D, released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The city survivor and two infected appearances are adapted from the [Validated Avatar Library for Inclusion and Diversity](https://github.com/xrtlab/Validated-Avatar-Library-for-Inclusion-and-Diversity---VALID) by Tiffany D. Do, Steve Zelenty, Mar Gonzalez-Franco and Ryan P. McMahan. The source avatars are Hispanic M1 Casual, White M2 Utility and Black F2 Casual from the [GLB conversion by c-frame](https://github.com/c-frame/valid-avatars-glb/tree/c539a28). Project Z adapted their bind poses, materials, and expressions and added animation. The original MIT notice follows.

Character motion is adapted from Quaternius's CC0 [Universal Animation Library](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html) and [Universal Animation Library 2](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary2.html). The clips were retargeted to the VALID characters and adjusted for the game's floor height and attack timing.

### Driving, vegetation and wildlife

- [Car Concept by Eric Chadwick at Darmstadt Graphics Group, based on Unity Fan's model](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CarConcept), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Adapted as the Carmine GT with revised paint, glass, steering, and door controls. Removed the logo plate and steering emblem.
- [Shrub 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_01), [Grass Medium 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_medium_01) and [Pine Sapling Small](https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_sapling_small) from Poly Haven, [CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Reduced the model geometry and embedded the 1K textures.
- [Fox by Bake3D](https://bake3d.ai/free-3d-models), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The in-game gait uses variable playback of the supplied walk loop.
- [Red Deer Stag by 3D Assets](https://3dassets.dev/assets/exotic-wildlife-hd-red-deer-stag-82f8d92a), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The stag mesh was skinned to the [deer rig and motion by the vsim contributors](https://github.com/kunalkushwaha/vsim/blob/main/packages/assets/library/CREDITS.md), copyright 2026, MIT. See the notice below.

The parked [Delivery Van](https://grab3d.com/models/vehicles/delivery-van/), [Minivan](https://grab3d.com/models/vehicles/minivan/), [City Bus](https://grab3d.com/models/vehicles/city-bus/), [1990s Hatchback](https://grab3d.com/models/vehicles/1990s-hatchback/), and [1990s Sedan](https://grab3d.com/models/vehicles/1990s-sedan/) from Grab3D are [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The game uses muted paint and deactivated lights on the bus and vans. The [Industrial Plastic Container](https://polyhaven.com/a/industrial_pastic_container) from Poly Haven is also [CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license).

### Reference environment materials

- The GLBs in `assets/reference_dressing/` came from the local Project Z Godot asset collection. They include the PZ-053 rail segment, PZ-059 metro carriage, PZ-062 station doors and platform bench, PZ-066 airport doors, PZ-068 control tower, PZ-070 baggage trolley, PZ-121 litter, PZ-126 water bottle, PZ-145 stretcher, PZ-154 turboprop, and PZ-172 concrete barrier. Their original project asset terms apply.
- [Worn Asphalt](https://polyhaven.com/a/worn_asphalt) by Amal Kumar supplies the road color, OpenGL normals, and roughness.
- [Qwantani Dusk 2 (Pure Sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/qwantani_dusk_2_puresky) by Greg Zaal and Jarod Guest and [Kloofendal Overcast (Pure Sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_overcast_puresky) by Greg Zaal supply the photographic skies. All three assets are [CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license).
