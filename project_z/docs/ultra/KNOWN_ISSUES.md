# Remaining work

- P06 and the shared city terrain have the strongest graphics coverage. Original imported facades and furniture still mix several authoring styles.
- Character changes are material adjustments on existing rigs. Dedicated facial, clothing and infected shaders and additional texture bakes remain open.
- Weather uses a simple shelter ray and exposure values. The puddles are shading masks on the road surface and have no collision or water depth.
- The water material represents the Hillview drainage channel. It uses a shallow opaque surface and normal ripples. Physical river flow and scene refraction remain open.
- Global illumination, expanded decal categories, a full authoring UI, and a batch texture conversion and migration workflow remain open.
- High includes costly effects and needs sustained hardware profiling before a performance target can be set.
